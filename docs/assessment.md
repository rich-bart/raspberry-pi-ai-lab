# Assessment / feedback rubric

All students can succeed without building a large program from scratch. Judge **investigation and explanations**, not only percentage accuracy.

| Category | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Computer and network | 20 | Booted Pi, received local IP, SSH and SCP completed |
| Data understanding | 20 | Correctly explained feature, label, train/test split |
| Working classifiers | 20 | Decision tree and Random Forest both produce results |
| Scientific comparison | 25 | Fair comparisons, matrix interpretation, explanation of noise/shift |
| Reflection and presentation | 15 | Team explains a change, what happened, and a limitation |
| **Total** | **100** | |

For formative assessment, ask: "What was your hypothesis? What single variable did you change? Did your results support it? What evidence is in the confusion matrix?" The hidden challenge's raw accuracy may be reported, but it should not outweigh the quality of the explanation.

Teams should use identifiers like `team07`; never put full student names or passwords in public repositories or submissions.
