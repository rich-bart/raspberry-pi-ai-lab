# Hardware and purchasing

**Per student:** Raspberry Pi 5 (4 GB recommended; 2 GB is sufficient for this exercise), CanaKit-compatible case and active cooling, manufacturer-rated Pi 5 USB-C power supply, >=32 GB microSD card preloaded with Raspberry Pi OS Desktop 64-bit, HDMI-capable portable display and its **separate power supply if necessary**, micro-HDMI video cable/adapter matched to the monitor, wired USB keyboard and mouse, 1 Cat5e/Cat6 Ethernet cable.

**Instructor:** one master Pi (Pi 5 with 4 GB recommended), its power and storage, wired keyboard/display for troubleshooting, 24-port **unmanaged Gigabit switch**, switch power supply, one Ethernet cable to the master, spare adapters, 2–3 spare SD cards and power outlets. For 15 students + teacher = **16 occupied ports**; 24 ports provide expansion.

Check monitor physical input, required cable type and power compatibility *before ordering in quantity*. USB-C video inputs on some portable monitors do **not** accept a Raspberry Pi's USB-C power connector as a display input; use the Pi's **micro-HDMI** output. Plan power strips based on the monitor + Pi separate power plugs and venue electrical rules. Label each Pi, cable, SD card and power adapter.

**Not needed:** router, wireless access point, Internet, GPU, paid model API, Raspberry Pi camera.

Teacher tip: buy **one full workstation first** and validate monitor boot, power, keyboard, network, training speed, and the entire offline lesson before ordering a class set.
